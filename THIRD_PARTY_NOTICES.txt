EXTERNAL OPTIONAL INTEGRATION
=============================

Blender
-------
Mesh3DViewer can locate an independently installed Blender executable and invoke
it externally to export .blend scenes to GLB. Blender is not declared as a
bundled Python/runtime dependency in requirements.txt.

If Blender itself is ever redistributed together with Mesh3DViewer, its GPL
license obligations must be handled separately. Merely calling a separately
installed Blender executable does not make Blender part of this distribution.
